## Overview

MyStack brand animation for landing page hero. Clean, professional SaaS aesthetic with modern gradients. Designed to communicate simplicity, reliability, and modern booking technology for Argentine businesses.

## Colors

- **Primary**: #1e293b (slate-800) - Headlines, important text
- **Accent Blue**: #4f46e5 (indigo-600) - Primary brand actions
- **Accent Purple**: #7c3aed (violet-600) - Gradient partner
- **Surface**: #ffffff - Cards, content areas
- **Surface Alt**: #f8fafc - Section backgrounds
- **Muted**: #64748b (slate-500) - Secondary text

## Typography

- **Headlines**: Inter 800, 64-120px depending on frame
- **Subhead**: Inter 600, 32-48px
- **Body**: Inter 400, 20-28px
- **Labels**: Inter 500, 16-20px

## Do's and Don'ts

- DO use gradient fills on accent elements
- DO keep layouts clean with generous whitespace
- DON'T use flat gray backgrounds - add texture or glows
- DON'T use more than 2 accent colors per scene
